About

Who is Chukwuebuka?

Chukwuebuka Ezeh is a Nigerian psychologist, researcher, entrepreneur and writer whose work is shaped by an interest in understanding people and building systems that improve their wellbeing and opportunities.

He graduated with First Class Honours in Psychology from the University of Ibadan and is pursuing an MSc in Health Psychology at Obafemi Awolowo University.

His work increasingly sits at the intersection of psychology, mental health, artificial intelligence, digital health, research and community development.

“I do not simply want to understand problems. I want to build systems that help solve them.”
